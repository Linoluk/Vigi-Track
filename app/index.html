    Data and settings

    Real reviews

    Vigi-Track ships with 5,550 real Drugs.com reviews of 37 common drugs, with their FDA labels. To study any of the 3,671 drugs, upload the full Drugs.com file from Kaggle or UCI (CSV or TSV).

    Upload a file

    Run outside Claude

    Opened this file on your computer? Paste an Anthropic API key to turn on the AI agent. It stays in this tab and is sent only to api.anthropic.com.

    AI reading limit

    For big drugs, the AI reads the most helpful reviews first. Keyword search always reads every review.

    Real data: the 150 most helpful reviews of each drug from the Drugs.com review dataset (UCI Machine Learning Repository, Gräßer et al. 2018, 215,063 reviews). The leaflet of each drug is the list of side effects named in its FDA label (openFDA, DailyMed), in the boxed warning, warnings and adverse reactions sections.

    The demo has 66 short reviews written for this project. The 5 drug names are invented. It is not real patient data.
